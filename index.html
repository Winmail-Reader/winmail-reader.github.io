FAQ

What is winmail.dat?

It's a TNEF file from Microsoft Outlook, often unreadable in other email clients like Apple Mail.

Does it support batch processing?

Yes, the Winmail Extractor pulls attachments from multiple winmail.dat files into your Downloads folder.

Can I preview before saving?

Quick Look lets you inspect any attachment directly within the app.

Is setup required?

No—simply drag winmail.dat files into Winmail Reader or open via right-click.
